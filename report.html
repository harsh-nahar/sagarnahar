What this archive includes

Public posts and pages, dates, authors, labels, original URLs, public comments, and available reply relationships. Public source responses and checksums are saved alongside the normalized data in the project.

Limits & omissions

  • Deleted, private, draft, moderated, and feed-omitted content cannot be recovered from public feeds.
  • Likes, reactions, follower lists, original themes, sidebar widgets, and platform accounts are not captured.
  • Feed counts matching does not prove that the platform exposes all content. Counts can change while crawling.
  • Comment reply relationships are retained when exposed by the source; inferred or unavailable relationships are not invented.
  • Supported YouTube, Vimeo, SoundCloud, and Internet Archive players stream only after a click; recordings are not downloaded. Provider tracking or ads may apply after loading. Other legacy/Flash players remain links and may be dead.
  • Empty layout paragraphs and excessive blank lines are normalized for reading; original source HTML remains unchanged in the saved archive data.
  • Only supported raster images of at most 20 MiB each are downloaded, subject to robots.txt. Missing images are labeled.
  • This is a read-only archive. Comment author names and public comment text are preserved; review before publishing.
  • The reading site selects credited writing from the configured personal blogs. Guest posts and excluded collaborative collections remain in the saved source data, not in this site or search. Source-capture counts below are unfiltered; published counts are reported separately.
  • Sharing and like widgets are omitted from reading and search; saved source HTML is unchanged. External web links open in a new tab.
